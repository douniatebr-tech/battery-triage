Gaussian process per chemistry, trained on lab-aged 18650 cells from Zhu et al. (2022, Nature Communications; data CC BY 4.0), with SoH measured against rated capacity and the 90% range calibrated on held-out cells. In tests on unseen cells, the SoH error was about 0.8–1.1 points for a 26-minute rest.
Research prototype for the ML triage study. For a chemistry not listed here, retest every battery until at least four cells of it have been fully tested (decision D-16). The rest only counts after a full charge, so the test still needs a charge.
